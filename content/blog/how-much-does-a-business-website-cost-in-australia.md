Nobody publishes this, so here it is. Ranges are AUD, 2026, for a real small-business site — not a landing page.

## The ranges

| Option | Typical cost | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DIY builder (Wix, Squarespace) | $0–$600/yr | Testing an idea |
| Freelancer, template-based | $1,500–$4,000 | Simple brochure site |
| Studio, custom build | $4,000–$12,000 | Service businesses that need leads |
| Shopify store | $6,000–$20,000 | Product brands |
| Agency retainer build | $20,000+ | Multi-team organisations |

Ongoing: domain ~$20/yr, hosting $0–$40/mo, Shopify plan from ~$50/mo, optional care plan $100–$400/mo.

## What actually drives the price

1. **Number of unique page templates** — not number of pages
2. **Content**: do you have copy and photos, or does someone have to write and shoot them?
3. **Integrations**: CRM, booking, payments, inventory
4. **SEO scope**: basic on-page vs local pages, schema and a content plan
5. **Deadline**: rushing costs more, everywhere

## Where people waste money

- Paying for a "custom" build that's a bought template with a logo swapped in
- Monthly retainers that only cover hosting
- Rebuilding after two years because nobody planned for editing
- Ads pointed at a site that converts at 0.4%

## What to ask before you sign

- Do I own the code, domain and hosting?
- What's the fixed price and fixed date?
- What's included after launch?
- Who writes the copy?
- What happens to my existing rankings? (If they can't answer this, walk.)
