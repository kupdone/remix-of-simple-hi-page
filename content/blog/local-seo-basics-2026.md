Most "local SEO guides" waste your time. Here's the short version of what actually still works in 2026.

## 1. Own your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile is the single highest-leverage thing you own. Free, fast, and it's what shows up in the map pack.

- Complete every field. All of them.
- Post weekly — offers, events, updates.
- Reply to every review, good or bad.

## 2. Fix your NAP everywhere

Name, Address, Phone. Same format. Everywhere.

| Directory | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | Critical |
| Apple Maps / Bing Places | High |
| Yelp, TripAdvisor, industry-specific | Medium |
| Random 2010-era directories | Skip |

## 3. Real reviews, on a schedule

You want a steady drip, not a spike. Ask every happy customer, and make it easy — a short link, a QR code on the receipt, a follow-up text.

:::warning
Do not buy reviews. Do not ask employees to leave reviews. Google catches it, and you'll lose the profile.
:::

## 4. Location-specific content

A homepage full of "we serve the greater metro area" is not location content. A page called `/best-coffee-shop-in-<neighbourhood>` with real photos, real hours, and a real map is.

```md
# Best coffee in Uttara, Dhaka

We're at House 12, Road 4 — two blocks from the Diabari roundabout.
Open 7am–10pm. Free wifi. Dog-friendly.
```

## 5. Speed and mobile

Google measures your site the same way it measures Amazon's. If your homepage takes 6 seconds on 4G, you lose — no matter how good your reviews are.

Run a free audit at [shinebetter.online/insights](/insights) to spot the basic website issues that usually hold local pages back.
:::

That's it. Do these five things well and you'll beat 90% of your local competition.
