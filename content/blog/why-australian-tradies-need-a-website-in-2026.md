Every tradie I talk to says the same thing: "I get all my work from word of mouth." That's true — right up until the referral pauses, googles your name, finds a Facebook page last updated in 2022, and quietly calls someone else.

## What actually happens after a referral

1. Someone gives your name to a mate
2. The mate googles "**your name** + your trade + your suburb"
3. They spend about eight seconds deciding whether you look legitimate
4. They call whoever looked most legitimate

A website doesn't replace word of mouth. It **stops leaking it**.

## What a 2026 tradie site has to do

- **Load in under two seconds on mobile data**, in a driveway, on three bars
- Show a **phone number and quote form above the fold**
- Have **one page per service**, not a single "Services" list — that's how Google ranks you for "hot water repair Perth" instead of just your business name
- Show **real photos of real jobs** with dates
- List **service areas** explicitly
- Carry **LocalBusiness schema** so hours, area and reviews are machine-readable

## The licensing and trust problem

Australian customers check. Put your licence number, insurance status and ABN on the page. It costs you nothing and removes the single biggest reason someone hesitates before calling a trade they've never used.

## Facebook is not a website

Your Facebook page is rented land: no control over reach, no SEO value pointing at you, and no ability to rank for the searches that convert. Use it for proof; own the destination.
