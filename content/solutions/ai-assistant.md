Most small businesses don't need "AI" — they need one calm assistant that never sleeps, never forgets a lead, and never sounds like a robot. That's what I'm building.

## What it does

- **Answers customer questions** on your site, WhatsApp and email — in your voice, not ChatGPT's
- **Qualifies leads** before they hit your inbox, so you only reply to real ones
- **Drafts marketing** — social captions, newsletters, replies — from your own brand guide
- **Books calls** straight into your calendar
- **Summarises** every conversation so you always know what happened

## What it is NOT

- A generic chatbot bolted onto your homepage
- A monthly SaaS you have to babysit
- A black box that scrapes your customer data

Currently in private beta with 4 studios and 2 local service businesses. If you want on the list, [reach out](/#contact) and I'll add you.
:::

Everything runs on your own accounts — OpenAI or Anthropic keys, your CRM, your calendar. You own the setup. I just wire it up.
