If your customers walk in through a door, they found you on Google Maps first. Let's make sure they do.

## What's in it

- **Google Business Profile** setup or rescue
- **Citation cleanup** — same NAP everywhere, no drift
- **Review strategy** — steady drip, real customers, no spammy nonsense
- **Location pages** — one per service area, written like a human wrote them
- **Local schema markup** — so Google actually understands what you do and where
