You don't need "AI strategy". You need the four things you do every week that shouldn't take a human, to stop taking a human.

## What I automate

- **Lead follow-up** — instant reply, qualification questions, routing to the right inbox
- **Quoting** — templated quotes generated from a form and sent for e-signature
- **Scheduling** — bookings straight into your calendar with reminders
- **Inbox triage** — summarise, categorise and draft replies in your voice
- **Reporting** — weekly numbers pulled from your CRM, ads and analytics into one email
- **Content repurposing** — one long piece into posts, captions and a newsletter

## How it's built

| Layer | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| Orchestration | n8n, Make, or plain server functions |
| Models | OpenAI or Anthropic, on your keys |
| Data | Your CRM, sheets, calendar, inbox |
| Guardrails | Human approval before anything customer-facing sends |

:::note
Everything runs on your accounts. You own the keys, the data and the workflows. If we stop working together, nothing switches off.
:::
