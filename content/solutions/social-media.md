Posting five times a week doesn't grow a business. Posting the *right* five things does. That's the whole job.

## What's included

- **Monthly content calendar** — planned around your launches, seasons, and offers
- **Captions & hooks** written in your voice (not "🚀 Excited to announce…")
- **On-brand creatives** — carousels, reels covers, story templates
- **Community management** — replies, DMs, comment triage (up to a set volume)
- **Monthly report** — what worked, what didn't, what we do next month

## Platforms I actively manage

- Instagram
- Facebook (Pages + Groups)
- LinkedIn (personal + company)
- TikTok (organic only)
- Pinterest (for e-commerce)

:::tip
Social is a compound game. The channels that look "slow" in month one usually carry you in month six. Don't quit at week three.
:::

## How it works

1. One 45-minute brand + voice call
2. I build a 30-day pilot calendar you approve in one round
3. Weekly Loom updates so you always know what's shipping
4. Month 2 onward we tune based on data, not vibes
