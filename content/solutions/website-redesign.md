A redesign is not a new coat of paint. It's a conversion project that happens to look better at the end.

## When a redesign is worth it

- Your site loads in more than three seconds on mobile
- It looks like it was built in 2016 (because it was)
- Traffic is fine, enquiries are not
- Nobody on your team can edit a single word without a developer
- You're on a page builder that fights you every time you touch it

## What I do

- **Audit first** — analytics, Search Console, Core Web Vitals, heatmap of what people actually click
- **Keep what ranks** — URL mapping and 301 redirects so you don't lose a decade of SEO
- **Rebuild the front end** — clean code, design tokens, real accessibility
- **Rewrite the money pages** — home, services, contact
- **Reinstate tracking** — GA4, Search Console, conversion events

:::warning
The fastest way to nuke your traffic is a redesign with no redirect map. Every redesign here ships with one.
:::
