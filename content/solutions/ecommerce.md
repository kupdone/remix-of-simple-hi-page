Most stores don't have a traffic problem. They have a **conversion** problem. Fixing it is usually cheaper than more ads.

## What I optimize

- **Product pages** — copy, imagery, above-the-fold, trust signals
- **Cart & checkout** — friction removal, abandoned-cart flows, upsells that don't feel gross
- **Speed** — Core Web Vitals, image pipeline, theme code cleanup
- **Search & filtering** — real search that finds what people meant
- **Analytics** — GA4 + server-side events so ad platforms actually get clean data

## Platforms

- **Shopify** (Liquid + Hydrogen)
- **WooCommerce** (only if you're already on it)
- **Custom / headless** (Next.js + Stripe or Shopify Storefront API)
