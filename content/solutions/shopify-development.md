Shopify handles payments, inventory and tax. Everything that makes a store feel like *your* brand is the part I build.

## What I do

- **Custom themes** — Liquid, Online Store 2.0 sections your team can rearrange
- **Headless storefronts** — Hydrogen or Next.js + Storefront API when you need full control
- **Speed work** — app audit, image pipeline, script deferral, Core Web Vitals
- **Product pages that sell** — copy, imagery, trust signals, above-the-fold clarity
- **Checkout & cart** — upsells, cart drawer, abandoned-cart flows
- **Migrations** — WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or legacy Shopify into a clean build
