Most channels don't have a content problem. They have a **discoverability** problem. That's what I fix.

## What I optimize

- **Titles & thumbnails** — tested, not guessed
- **Metadata** — descriptions, tags, chapters, end screens
- **Playlists** — structured so YouTube keeps recommending you
- **Analytics** — CTR, retention, browse-vs-search — the numbers that actually matter

- Full channel audit (10-page PDF)
- 30-day action plan
- Optimized titles + descriptions for your last 10 videos
- Thumbnail templates you can reuse
- Monthly check-in for the first 90 days

:::tip
YouTube SEO is a compound game. Six months of doing this right beats one viral video every time.
