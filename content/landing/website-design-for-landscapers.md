Most landscaping businesses lose work before the phone ever rings. Someone searches, lands on a slow page with a 2019 gallery and no pricing, and taps back to a competitor. A website for landscapers has exactly one job: turn a stranger searching at 9pm into a booked job tomorrow morning.

## What landscapers actually need

- **A phone number and quote form above the fold** — on mobile, where 80% of your traffic is
- **Service pages, not one "Services" page** — one page per job you do, so Google can rank each
- **Real photos of real garden and outdoor transformations** — stock imagery reads as fake within a second
- **Reviews on the page**, not just on Google
- **Sub-2-second load** — every extra second costs you enquiries
- **LocalBusiness and Service schema** so your service area, hours and pricing are machine-readable
